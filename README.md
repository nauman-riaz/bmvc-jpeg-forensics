# Mistaking Periodicity for Manipulation

**JPEG-Induced Structural Bias in Document Tampering Detection**

Nauman Riaz · BMVC 2026 conference presentation

[Open the presentation](https://nauman-riaz.github.io/bmvc-jpeg-forensics/)

[Download the presentation PDF](BMVC-2026-Mistaking-Periodicity-for-Manipulation.pdf) (31 pages, one per slide or reveal).

The 16-slide browser presentation includes staged animations, a Q&A appendix, and a presenter view with per-reveal speaker notes and an elapsed timer.

- Use the arrow keys or Space to advance slides and reveals at your own pace. The opening receipt stays unmarked until you advance.
- **T** shows or hides the rehearsal toolbar.
- **S** opens the presenter window with narration and a timer.
- **F** enters fullscreen; **O** opens the overview; **C** or **End** returns to the closing slide.

The speaker notes are public, as are the slides. Share only the audience window during the talk.

All images, fonts and libraries are included locally. To preview locally, run `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder and open `http://127.0.0.1:8000/`.

Source images are credited in the slides and speaker notes. Third-party assets retain their respective rights; Reveal.js retains its bundled license.
