Metric definitions

Detection Failure Rate (DFR)

At each IoU cutoff, measure the share of images whose predicted and annotated tampered regions overlap at or below the cutoff. Average the six rates at cutoffs from 0 to 0.5 in increments of 0.1.

False Positive Area (FPA)

For each image, divide the number of pixels falsely flagged as tampered by the full image area. Average that fraction across images.

FFDN ablation

Pixel F1 (%) before and after PRC, with and without the Wavelet-like Frequency Enhancement module. The Vision Enhancement module remains in both configurations.
